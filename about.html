About / Faniel Yemane

Curiosity,
made practical.

I like building things that bring software and hardware together—whether that's a camera that recognises objects, a game running on an FPGA, or a Raspberry Pi controlling a physical mechanism.

My background

Learning
by building.

My electrical engineering projects at the British Columbia Institute of Technology have given me opportunities to work across software, electronics, and digital hardware. I enjoy taking an idea through implementation and seeing how the pieces behave together.

For the ARMI Bot team capstone, my work focused on the graphical interface, machine-learning integration, and object detection. That project brought together my interests in computer vision and interfaces that make complex systems easier to understand.

Other projects have taken me from C++ applications in Windows to Raspberry Pi and Linux, from camera-based colour sorting to digital game logic. I also explore algorithms and machine learning through personal projects and coursework.
